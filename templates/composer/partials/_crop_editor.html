{% load i18n static %} {% comment %} The image editor: the window that crops one image, with the aspect-ratio presets. It sits inside a page's Alpine component, which takes the editor's state and gestures from ``window.cropEditorBase()`` (spread into it) and says itself what saving does, in its own ``saveCrop()`` — the composer swaps the copy into the post, the idea window into the idea. A component may also replace ``cropFormatNote()`` to say what the crop applies to. Opened with ``openCrop({ url, ... })``. Context: crop_layer (optional z-index class, when the window has to sit above another one). {% endcomment %}
