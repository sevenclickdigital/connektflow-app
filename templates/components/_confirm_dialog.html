{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The app's confirmation dialog, in place of the browser's confirm(). Included once by base.html. It answers two things: - htmx `hx-confirm="Question?"`: the request goes out only after "Confirm". - a plain form with `data-confirm="Question?"`: it submits only after "Confirm". Optional on the same element: `data-confirm-label` (the confirm button's text) and `data-confirm-tone="danger"` (red icon and button, for deletions). {% endcomment %}
