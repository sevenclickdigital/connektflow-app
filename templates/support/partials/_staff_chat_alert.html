{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The live chat's alert across the administrative area: every few seconds it asks how many chats wait in the queue and how many messages the agent has not read (support_staff:chat_pulse), shows the sum on the menu's "Live chat" badge and, when either grows, plays a short sound and shows a toast. The console (support/staff_chat_console.html) marks the page with data-support-console and alerts on its own, opening the chat's window. The sound (window.cfSupportBeep, also used by the console) is made with the Web Audio API, no file to load. Browsers only let a page play sound after the person interacted with it, so the audio is unlocked on the first click or key press. {% endcomment %}
