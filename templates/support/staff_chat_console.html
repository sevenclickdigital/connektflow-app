Right, for the team only: who the visitor is, their browser, system and device, the page they are on now and the pages they went through. While this page is open the agent counts as online (each poll marks their presence), so the widget offers the chat instead of a ticket. It polls the lists and the open chat every few seconds, even in a background tab, and when a chat joins the queue or a visitor writes in one of the agent's chats it plays a sound (window.cfSupportBeep, support/partials/_staff_chat_alert.html), shows a browser notification if allowed and, with no chat open, opens that one. Messages are escaped before their formatting marks become tags (cfMessageFormat.render, support/partials/_message_format.html). The console fits the window (column_height / content_scroll, as the inbox does): the three panels keep the window's height and each scrolls on its own, so a long conversation scrolls inside the chat window instead of growing the page.
