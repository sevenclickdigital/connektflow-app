{% extends "base.html" %} {% load i18n billing_extras %} {% comment %} The contracting modal, shown right after a workspace is created. A real page styled as a modal rather than markup injected into every layout: it is arrived at by redirect from workspace creation, it survives a reload after a failed gateway call, and it can be linked to from the billing page. The dim backdrop and the single centred card give it the "you have one decision to make" feel of a modal without depending on a parent page having opened it. Two states. Before contracting it asks for the decision (and, the first time, the payer's CPF or CNPJ). After it, while the payment is not confirmed, it offers the invoice and asks the server every few seconds whether the workspace is active yet, leaving for the calendar when it is.
