{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The second step of the connect guide for a platform connected by a form of our own (Bluesky, Mastodon, DEV.to): its fields, posted with HTMX to the platform's connect view. A refusal comes back as this same partial with ``error``; a success answers with HX-Redirect (the account list, or Mastodon's server). Mastodon's form is also ``data-oauth-popup``: on a desktop its server opens in the same small window as the other platforms' sign-in (_oauth_popup.html). Mastodon asks for the server only, never the username, which the person types on the server's own sign-in page. Most accounts live on mastodon.social, so the form first offers to sign in there with one click (the button carries the server as its value) and shows the field only for "another server" (Alpine ``other``; the field is disabled while hidden, so it is not sent). Bluesky offers the same two ways in: a bsky.social username, to which the view adds the domain, or a full handle on the person's own domain (Alpine ``mode``), then the app password in both.
