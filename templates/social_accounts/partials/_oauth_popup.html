{% comment %} Runs a connection in a small window instead of taking the page away. A form marked ``data-oauth-popup`` is posted into a popup, with ``popup=1`` so the server ends the round trip on connect_done.html (views.finish_in_popup). That page announces, over a same-origin BroadcastChannel, where the flow continues; this page goes there, which is either itself reloaded with the new account or the account picker. A form may also carry ``data-popup-action``: the address to post to when the popup opens, for flows that take their return address in the URL (the Google sign-in on the login and signup pages). The popup is skipped, and the form submits as it always did, on touch devices and narrow screens, where a second window is more trouble than help, and when the browser blocks it. {% endcomment %}
