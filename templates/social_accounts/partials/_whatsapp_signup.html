{% comment %} WhatsApp Embedded Signup. Any element with ``data-whatsapp-signup`` opens Meta's popup. Meta answers twice and in no fixed order: the FB.login callback carries the authorization code, and a ``WA_EMBEDDED_SIGNUP`` window message carries the WhatsApp Business Account and phone number picked. Once both are in, the hidden form posts them to ``connect_whatsapp``, which verifies the ids against the token before connecting anything.
