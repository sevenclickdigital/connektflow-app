{% comment %} WhatsApp Embedded Signup. Any element with ``data-whatsapp-signup`` opens Meta's popup. Meta answers twice and in no fixed order: the FB.login callback carries the authorization code, and a ``WA_EMBEDDED_SIGNUP`` window message carries the WhatsApp Business Account and phone number picked. Once both are in, the hidden form posts them to ``connect_whatsapp``, which verifies the ids against the token before connecting anything. The flow version is ``signup_version`` (providers.whatsapp.EMBEDDED_SIGNUP_VERSION). From v5 the session object names the account in ``messaging_account_ids`` (the former ``waba_id``), and onboarding a WhatsApp Business app number is on by default; it is switched off here because such a number must not be registered on the Cloud API, which connecting does. Context: ``whatsapp_signup`` (app_id, config_id, version, signup_version, messages), ``workspace_id``. {% endcomment %} {{ whatsapp_signup|json_script:"whatsapp-signup-config" }}
